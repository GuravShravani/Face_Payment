What is GST?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a comprehensive indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India. It has replaced multiple cascading taxes levied by the central and state governments.

GST Registration

Businesses with turnover exceeding ₹40 lakhs (₹20 lakhs for special category states) must register for GST. Registration can be done online through the GST portal.

GST Returns

Regular taxpayers must file monthly/quarterly returns including GSTR-1 (outward supplies), GSTR-3B (summary return), and annual return GSTR-9.
